# GTM Director JD Patterns

## Takeaways

### What every role asks for
- Every family puts team building at #1: PMM 75%, Demand Gen 93%, CS 85%, RevOps 67%
- But the hire inherits no team in many cases: 26 of 53 JDs lean builder (hands on, first hire, player coach)
- The gap: companies want a leader eventually and a doer now. The doer half is what an agent plus consultant can fill on day one

### AI is now a line item
- 32 of 53 JDs (60%) ask the hire to personally use AI tools or build automations
- 39 of 53 (74%) mention AI, agents or automation anywhere (includes marketing automation)
- Named tools: Claude, Claude Code, ChatGPT, Cursor, n8n, Clay
- Sharpest asks: Morning Consult "Ship AI agents and automations across scoring, routing, enrichment, QA, and reporting"; DISQO "Build custom AI skills, agents, and artifacts"; Rillet "AI-native mindset using tools like Claude to automate workflows"
- POV: buyers already believe agents can do GTM work. You are not selling the idea, only proof that you can run it

### Stage changes the buyer
- Raised under $50M: 71% of JDs lean builder
- Raised $100M+: 22% lean builder, 78% name pipeline as the metric
- POV: early stage buyers want the work done. Later stage buyers want the number moved. Pitch deliverables to the first group and pipeline impact to the second

### Most agent doable work by family (from the top 15 themes)
- PMM: positioning and messaging framework (69%), value stories from technical features (69%), sales toolkit and battlecards (50%), category narrative (50%)
- Demand Gen: full funnel reporting and conversion diagnostics (80%), AI in demand gen workflows (60%), ABM programs (60%), attribution frameworks (53%)
- CS: playbooks and operating rhythm (69%), QBR and success plan templates (62%), voice of customer synthesis into roadmap (85%)
- RevOps: GTM AI agents and automations (67%), board and exec revenue reporting (56%), territory and segmentation design (44%), capacity and quota models (44%)

### What an agent cannot replace
- Team leadership, executive presence, stakeholder alignment and domain years rank in the top 5 of every family
- POV: position SandsDX as the bridge until the hire lands, or as the reason they hire one level lower

### Data limits
- 53 JDs from 42 companies; RevOps has only 9 JDs, so its shares are directional
- Funding band is a proxy for stage; Clay gives total raised, not round
- Themes were grouped by Claude agents from paraphrased bullets; every row traces to `data/requirements.csv` and the source JD
