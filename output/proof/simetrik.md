**US ICP used for this list (inference from the evidence above)**
- Company type: PSP, acquirer, cross border payments or remittance, card issuer, neobank or embedded finance, high volume marketplace, B2B payments
- Volume: millions of transactions a month or $1B+ processed a year, across 3+ banks, processors or rails
- Size: about 400 to 3,500 employees (Clay). Big enough for a dedicated finance ops team, small enough that reconciliation is still spreadsheets plus ERP plus scripts (inference)
- Fit boosters: LatAm corridors (Simetrik's home market and reference base), a new rail (stablecoin, RTP, own bank charter), or M&A that merges two ledgers
- Disqualifiers: known Simetrik customers (above), dLocal, Stax, Possible Finance; global megacaps where Simetrik has no reference yet
- Excluded on purpose: Billtrust, Tipalti (they sell reconciliation adjacent products; partner more than buyer), Toast (8,075 employees, outside size band)

## 7. How an agent keeps this list fresh weekly (spec)

**JD lines it serves**
- "ABM account penetration: Tier-1 engagement, informed by intent signals and content."
- "Bias for AI-native execution (AI workflows, AI content/SEO/research/account intelligence)"
- "Iteratively refine performance using dashboards and weekly data."

**Spec**
- **Schedule**: every Monday 6am ET, one run
- **Inputs**: the 25 accounts plus a Clay saved search (US HQ, payments, fintech or marketplace descriptions, 400 to 3,500 employees)
- **Step 1, trigger scan**: for each account, web and news search for the last 7 days on: acquisition, merger, funding, bank charter, license, new country, stablecoin, RTP or FedNow, new processor partnership, CFO or Controller hire, finance ops job posts (Clay jobs filter: "reconciliation", "payment operations", "revenue accounting")
- **Step 2, new account discovery**: rerun the Clay search, drop known customers and current list, add up to 5 new accounts that match the ICP with a sourced trigger
- **Step 3, rescore**: apply the section 4 criteria; promote to Tier 1 at 5 or more, demote any Tier 1 account with no trigger in 9 months
- **Step 4, intent merge**: pull first party signals from HubSpot or Salesforce (site visits, webinar attendance) and any intent tool the team runs; flag accounts with both a trigger and intent
- **Step 5, output**: update a Google Sheet (account, tier, trigger, source link, date) and post a 10 line Slack digest to the VP GTM: promotions, new accounts, and the one suggested touch per promoted account
- **Guardrails**: public sources only, every trigger carries a link and date, no contact data pulled, no outreach sent by the agent
- **Effort**: about 1 to 2 hours to set up, then minutes a week of review (estimate)
