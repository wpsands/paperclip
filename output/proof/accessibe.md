### 2b. Reputation issues demand gen has to plan around

- **FTC order (2025)**: the FTC finalized an order on 2025-04-21 requiring accessiBe to pay $1 million. The order bars claims that its automated tools can make any website WCAG compliant or keep it compliant over time, unless backed by evidence ([FTC](https://ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/04/ftc-approves-final-order-requiring-accessibe-pay-1-million), [National Law Review](https://natlawreview.com/article/ftc-settles-accessibe-misleading-statements-about-wcag-compliance))
- **Overlay criticism**: the National Federation of the Blind revoked accessiBe's convention sponsorship in 2021 ([Level Access summary](https://www.levelaccess.com/lies-damned-lies-overlays-and-widgets/)). Arizona State University's accessibility office advises against overlays ([ASU](https://accessibility.asu.edu/overlays)). The Overlay Fact Sheet open letter has hundreds of signers (counts vary by source, unverified)
- **Lawsuit data used against overlays**: about 22.6% of H1 2025 suits targeted sites with an overlay installed, per UsableNet midyear as cited secondhand ([TestParty](https://testparty.ai/blog/accessibility-overlay-lawsuits-data), [Silktide](https://silktide.com/s/overlays-got-1416-sites-sued-in-2025/)). Note: TestParty and Silktide are competitors
- **Competitor content attacks the brand in search**: a "does accessiBe make my website ADA compliant" search returns the [FTC complaint exhibit](https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/ftc_gov/pdf/2223156accessibecomplaintexn.pdf) and a Capterra review where an auditor says it will not ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/212877/accessiBe/reviews/)). TestParty runs a page series titled "Why 800+ Businesses with AccessiBe Were Still Sued" ([TestParty](https://testparty.ai/blog/why-800-businesses-with-accessibe-were-still-sued))
- **Point of view**: the old demand gen engine bought clicks on lawsuit fear and promised compliance. That message is now a legal risk and a search liability. The flat budget has to move from "compliance in one line of code" toward proof based messages: Gartner placement, accessFlow for developers, partner delivery, and usability for people with disabilities. This is a constraint for the plan, not a crisis to hide

## 5. 90 day plan

Budget assumption: $250K/month held flat (per JD). All targets below are **estimates** to be reset against the real baseline in week 1. Internal data (CAC, CVR, pipeline by channel) is not public.

### Segments

| Segment | Product | Primary channels | Core message (FTC safe) |
|---|---|---|---|
| SMB self serve | accessWidget | Google Search, Meta, YouTube, Shopify app, email nurture | "Improve usability for visitors with disabilities and start your WCAG work today" plus proof (reviews, G2) |
| Agencies and partners | Partner Program (referral, reseller, white label) | LinkedIn, webinars, email, partner directories | "Add an accessibility revenue line before EAA and Title II deadlines" |
| Mid market | accessFlow, Code Agent, managed services | LinkedIn, Google non brand, webinars, Gartner content | "Gartner Challenger. Fix issues in code where developers work" |

### Days 1 to 30: audit and stop the leaks

- **Agency and vendor review**: scorecard each media agency on CPA trend, test velocity, reporting quality and fees as % of spend. Decide keep, restructure or consolidate by day 30 (JD: "holding partners accountable")
- **Claims and compliance sweep**: audit every ad, LP and email against the FTC order language. Remove "ensure compliance" style claims. Replace with substantiated proof
- **Measurement baseline**: UTM and offline conversion audit, CRM source mapping with RevOps, one weekly report (spend, CAC, trials, pipeline by segment and channel)
- **Reallocate 10% of spend into a test fund** (about $25K/month): taken from the lowest ROAS ad groups found in the audit
- **Brand SERP defense**: publish owned pages for "accessiBe pricing", "accessiBe reviews", "is accessiBe compliant" (honest FAQ citing the FTC order outcome), and "accessiBe vs AudioEye / UserWay"
- **KPIs** (estimates): 100% of live ads reviewed for claims; agency scorecard done; baseline CAC by channel documented; 4 owned brand pages live

### Days 31 to 60: build the partner and deadline engines

- **Partner Program acquisition campaign**: LinkedIn and email to web agencies on Shopify, WordPress and Webflow; Clay list of agencies by tech stack and client vertical. Offer: partner tier benefits launched 2026-10-14
- **Webinar series (2 sessions)**: "EAA one year in: what enforcement looks like" for e-commerce, and "Title II April 2027: a plan for agencies serving public sector". Promote through paid social, partners and email; recycle into SEO pages and short YouTube clips
- **SMB nurture rebuild**: 5 email trial nurture with segment branches (Shopify, WordPress, other); add holdouts to measure incrementality
- **CRO tests**: 3 A/B tests on pricing and trial pages (plan selector by traffic, proof blocks with Gartner and G2, trial CTA)
- **Paid structure**: separate brand, competitor, problem ("ADA website", "EAA compliance") and product campaigns; cut overlapping keywords between agencies
- **KPIs** (estimates): 150+ net new partner applications; 600+ webinar registrants; trial to paid conversion +10% vs baseline; blended CAC flat with 10% budget in tests

### Days 61 to 90: scale winners and hand over

- **Scale**: move the test fund into the top 2 winning tests; stop the bottom quartile of ad groups
- **Mid market motion**: LinkedIn ABM to companies with EU e-commerce exposure and public sector suppliers; accessFlow and Code Agent demo offer; Gartner placement in creative
- **AEO**: re-run the 6 query proxy plus 20 more prompts monthly; track named vs cited share against AudioEye, UserWay and Level Access
- **Attribution**: incrementality tests on Meta and YouTube (geo or holdout) so the returning head inherits measured channels
- **Handover pack**: channel playbook, agency structure, test log and next 90 day backlog for when the permanent head returns
- **KPIs** (estimates): pipeline from partners 15% of new pipeline; blended CAC down 10% on flat spend; accessibe.com cited in 3 of 6 proxy queries; documented test log of 12+ tests
