## Job description
About ArmorCode

ArmorCode is the agentic control plane for Unified Exposure Management: one platform that unifies, prioritizes, and remediates risk across applications, code, software supply chain, cloud, infrastructure, and AI.

The ArmorCode Agentic AI Platform ingests findings from 400+ security tools, correlates them with business context in the Context Risk Graph, and puts Anya AI agents to work prioritizing, routing, and verifying fixes. 

The platform is proven at enterprise scale, processing 400B+ findings annually and supporting more than 5,600 security professionals and 300,000 developers. Global enterprises including Visa, PayPal, Volvo Group, and Johnson Controls use ArmorCode to shorten find-to-fix time and reduce risk.

About the Role

As Director, Customer Success, you will own the end-to-end customer journey for ArmorCode's enterprise security customers — from onboarding and adoption through renewal, expansion, and advocacy. You'll lead a global organization spanning Customer Success (US and India), setting the strategy, playbooks, and operational rigor that turn customers into long-term partners and champions of the ArmorCode platform. You will bring an AI-first mindset to the role, leveraging automation and AI-driven insights to proactively engage customers, accelerate adoption, and uncover expansion opportunities.

This is a hands-on leadership role for someone who thrives in a fast-moving, scale-up environment and is equally comfortable setting strategy with the C-suite and rolling up their sleeves on the details that drive GRR, NRR, and customer trust.

What You'll Own

Customer Retention & Growth

- Own and improve GRR and NRR, setting clear playbooks and KPIs.

- Partner with Sales on expansion and renewal strategy, driving upsell and cross-sell motions.

- Design and execute proactive engagement strategies to identify and capitalize on expansion opportunities early in the customer lifecycle.

- Establish C-level engagement frameworks to ensure customers view ArmorCode as a strategic partner.

Customer Success Management

- Own the global Customer Success strategy and operations, ensuring world-class responsiveness, resolution times, and customer satisfaction.

- Define and track SLAs (first response, time-to-resolution, backlog management) and ensure performance meets or exceeds benchmarks.

- Implement scalable support models (tiered support, self-service, knowledge base, AI/automation).

- Build escalation paths that ensure seamless collaboration between Support, Engineering, and Customer Success.

- Drive continuous improvement by analyzing support tickets to identify systemic issues and feeding insights into Product and CS.

- Drive operational excellence in ticket resolution efficiency and turnaround time, continuously identifying opportunities to improve.

Customer Onboarding and Training

- Develop scalable onboarding and adoption programs that ensure time-to-value and deepen product usage.

- Develop the training and adoption strategy for customers.

Product Adoption and Value Realization

- Develop and implement a product communication cadence and strategy, keeping clients updated on product updates, cybersecurity trends, and industry insights.

- Drive new feature adoption and manage release planning alongside Engineering and Product.

- Define metrics and dashboards to monitor adoption health across accounts.

- Utilize customer data and analytics to assess customer behavior, usage patterns, and overall satisfaction, building the right adoption and product-usage dashboards.

- Spearhead proactive adoption campaigns, utilizing predictive modeling and AI-driven alerts to intervene before churn risks materialize.

Customer Advocacy and Community

- Build formal advocacy programs — advisory boards, case studies, references, and peer communities.

- Ensure customers become champions within their industries and referenceable for new sales opportunities.

Cross-Functional Leadership

- Serve as the voice of the customer to executive leadership, influencing product, sales, and marketing strategy.

- Partner with Product and Engineering to influence roadmap priorities based on customer needs and adoption blockers.

- Drive product roadmap through a mix of customer support data analytics and customer feedback loops.

- Work with Finance to forecast retention and expansion revenue.

- Partner with Marketing on advocacy.

Team Leadership

- Lead a team of Customer Success team members (US and India).

- Ensure a motivated, collaborative, and well-connected team across continents.

- Create repeatable processes for enterprise account management, QBRs, and executive engagement.

- Champion an AI-first mindset within the team, training and enabling CSMs to leverage AI tools and methodologies for improved efficiency and customer outcomes.
