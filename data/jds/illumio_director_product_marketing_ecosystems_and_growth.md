Product Marketing at Illumio

The Product Marketing team at Illumio focuses on understanding the customer, technology, the ecosystem, market drivers, competition and customer needs with the responsibility of communicating product messaging and value to internal and external audiences. You will be part of a team responsible for developing positioning and differentiated messaging, defining go-to-market strategy, launching new solutions and driving thought leadership for the Illumio solutions. You will 'roll up your sleeves', 'dig in', learn, problem solve, be creative and do what it takes to win the market.

The Role

Ecosystems and Growth are two of Illumio's most important accelerants to pipeline and time-to-value: our technology and cloud alliance partnerships extend our reach into other vendors' install bases, and our Product Led Growth (PLG) motion turns Web Trial and Marketplace experiences into a fast, self-serve path to value for prospects and customers.

As Director, Product Marketing, Ecosystems & Growth, you will lead both disciplines as one connected strategy — building a bench that thinks about partner-led and product-led growth as a single system rather than two separate motions. You will lead a team of three: a Product Marketing Manager driving our strategic technology and cloud alliances, a Product Marketing Manager driving our Growth/PLG trial and marketplace experience, and a Technical Marketing Manager providing the technical depth behind both. You will report to the Head of Product Marketing and represent Ecosystems & Growth in cross-functional and executive planning.

This role also serves as the technical arm of product marketing. You are not just directing technical work through your Technical Marketing Manager — you are personally credible in front of a technical audience, comfortable architecting and building a compelling product demo yourself, and able to step in and own technical proof points for a launch, a partner integration, or a Trial experience when it matters.

YOUR IMPACT:

 - Strategy and vision – Set the strategic direction for Illumio's Ecosystems & Growth practice, unifying alliance-led and product-led growth motions into a coherent plan for accelerating pipeline and customer time-to-value.

 - Technical demo ownership – Architect and build the product demos that prove Illumio's value to partners, prospects, and the field — for joint alliance integrations, for the Trial and Marketplace experience, and for major launches. Set the technical bar the team's demo environments and assets are held to, and be hands-on building or rebuilding a demo yourself when the moment calls for it.

 - Alliance marketing leadership – Own the joint messaging, positioning, and value propositions for Illumio's key technology and cloud partners, and ensure partner campaigns, co-sell enablement, and joint launches land with impact.

 - Growth and PLG leadership – Direct the strategy for the Web Trial and Marketplace experience — the journeys, landing pages, nurtures, and trial assets that turn prospects into active users — and guide your team through execution.

 - Team leadership and development – Lead, coach, and grow a team of three product marketers across alliance and growth disciplines, setting priorities, raising the bar on craft, and building their paths to the next level.

 - Cross-functional and executive partnership – Act as the senior marketing voice to Business Development, Partner Sales, Product Management, and the Office of the CTO, and represent Ecosystems & Growth priorities at the leadership table.

 - Integrated go-to-market planning – Build and direct integrated GTM plans that generate demand and pipeline through both alliance channels and PLG motions, tracking performance and reallocating investment based on results.

 - Launch integration – Ensure every major product launch has both an alliance/partner motion and a Trial/Marketplace motion built in from the start, in partnership with the broader product marketing team.

 - Market intelligence – Stay ahead of partner ecosystem trends, PLG and trial best practices from other vendors, and the competitive landscape, and translate that intelligence into strategy your team can act on.
