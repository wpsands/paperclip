ABOUT THE ROLE

As the Director of Demand Generation, US, you will build and own Simetrik's US inbound demand engine from the ground up. This role is designed for how enterprise CFOs, COOs, Controllers, and Heads of Payments buy in the US: self-educating through search and content, engaging via webinars and executive conversations, and converting on a website optimized for their time. Your core job: transform this behavior into a predictable, compounding pipeline engine—not paid ads or one-off events.

You will own inbound and demand-gen surfaces for simetrik.com, SEO and content-led demand, marketing automation and lead nurture, the MQL engine and handoff to Sales, webinars and digital programs, and organic LinkedIn. You will run a focused field and events motion, ABM for tier-1 named accounts, an executive community, and PR—unified under one pipeline metric. You will report directly to the Vice President of GTM.

Initially, this is a hands-on IC role. You will execute leveraging shared services (Brand, Content, Creative, GTM Engineering, Sales) and agencies. Success in the first year sets the foundation for scaling a demand team around your function.
