ABOUT THE ROLE

GC AI is expanding across workflows, pricing tiers, and customer segments. We are hiring a Director of Product Marketing to lead the function and own narrative, positioning, pricing, packaging, launches, enablement, and competitive strategy.

You will build the systems and standards needed to scale product marketing. You will partner with Product, Sales, Customer Success, Revenue Operations, and Marketing to bring every product, feature, and tier to market with clarity and measurable impact.

This role reports to the CMO and combines strategic leadership with hands-on execution.

WHAT YOU’LL DO

LEAD AND SCALE PRODUCT MARKETING

 - Set the product marketing strategy, priorities, operating rhythm, and quality standards

 - Define the team structure and hire, mentor, and develop product marketers

 - Create clear ownership across positioning, launches, enablement, pricing, and market intelligence

 - Align Product, Sales, Customer Success, Revenue Operations, and Marketing around shared priorities and measures

SHAPE PRODUCT STRATEGY

 - Use customer, usage, market, and competitive signals to influence roadmap decisions

 - Connect market dynamics and customer needs to internal product priorities

 - Give leadership clear recommendations on workflows, segments, and growth opportunities

OWN POSITIONING AND NARRATIVE

 - Define differentiated positioning across product lines, customer segments, and channels

 - Connect product messaging to GC AI’s broader Legal AI category narrative

 - Translate complex legal and AI capabilities into precise messaging for in-house lawyers

 - Maintain consistency across the website, sales materials, product surfaces, and customer education

PRODUCT LAUNCH LEADERSHIP AND MANAGEMENT

 - Coordinate cross-functional launch teams, own timelines, and maintain launch plans with milestones, dependencies, decision owners, and readiness criteria

 - Define and execute go-to-market strategy, including target segments, positioning, messaging, enablement, channel plans, and launch communications

 - Track post-launch adoption, revenue, customer feedback, and funnel performance, then lead reviews and prioritize product and go-to-market improvements

OWN PRICING AND PACKAGING

 - Lead pricing, packaging, and monetization strategy across product tiers

 - Use customer research, usage data, willingness-to-pay analysis, and competitive insight to guide recommendations

 - Partner with Product, Finance, Sales, and leadership on pricing and packaging decisions

EQUIP SALES AND CUSTOMER SUCCESS

 - Deliver talk tracks, decks, competitive briefs, objection guidance, and training

 - Partner with Sales leadership on deal strategy and positioning

 - Maintain current enablement materials for each product tier, segment, and priority workflow

 - Measure whether enablement improves confidence, consistency, and win rates

LEAD CUSTOMER AND MARKET INTELLIGENCE

 - Run structured customer research and win-loss programs

 - Maintain a current view of competitors, category shifts, and buyer expectations

 - Turn findings into go-to-market, pricing, roadmap, and deal strategy recommendations

BUILD CATEGORY LEADERSHIP

 - Lead analyst engagement, competitive positioning, and external thought leadership

 - Build relationships with relevant analysts and market voices

 - Develop a credible point of view on Legal AI and the changing work of in-house legal teams

DEVELOP REVENUE-DRIVING CONTENT

 - Oversee case studies, return-on-investment stories, comparisons, and proof points across the funnel

 - Partner with Brand and Demand Generation on content that supports pipeline, adoption, and expansion

 - Ensure customer-facing content is accurate, useful, and grounded in evidence

MEASURE AND IMPROVE

 - Define product marketing metrics tied to revenue, adoption, expansion, and win rates

 - Establish baselines and reporting for launches, messaging, enablement, and competitive performance

 - Use results to refine strategy and allocate resources
