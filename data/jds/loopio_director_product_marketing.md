## Job description
TAKE YOUR CAREER TO NEW HEIGHTS WITH LOOPIO! 🚀✨

Loopio is looking for a Director of Product Marketing to lead product marketing strategy and execution for our most important initiatives. As our most senior PMM, you’ll set the direction for pricing, go-to-market product strategy, and competitive positioning across the portfolio, partnering closely with Product, Sales, and Marketing leadership to translate product strategy into market-winning plans. This role backfills a critical leadership position on the Product Growth & Experience team and reports directly to the VP, Product Growth & Experience. This is a high-visibility, high-autonomy opportunity for someone with proven success scaling a B2B SaaS company who wants to shape how Loopio wins in a competitive, AI-driven market.

Note: This is an existing vacancy on the team 

💻 WHAT YOU’LL BE DOING

 - Own the narrative for Loopio’s evolution into a multi-product, AI-native portfolio, translating capabilities across product lines into a single, coherent market position

 - Set go-to-market strategy for major product launches and strategic initiatives, aligning Product, Sales, Customer Experience, and Marketing around a single narrative and launch plan

 - Partner closely with Product, Finance, and GTM on pricing strategy, shaping structure, packaging, and value metrics to drive adoption and expansion revenue

 - Shape competitive positioning and win/loss analysis, turning market and customer insight into messaging, battlecards, and sales enablement that sales teams actually use

 - Partner with the senior leadership team on long-term product and market strategy, representing the voice of the customer and the market in major planning cycles

 - Mentor and provide strategic guidance to other product marketers, raising the bar for product marketing craft across the team

 - Act as a trusted advisor to product team peers on roadmap prioritization, bringing market and competitive context to major build decisions

 - Represent Loopio externally as a thought leader and spokesperson for our positioning, through analyst briefings, customer conversations, and industry events

✨ WHAT YOU’LL BRING TO THE TEAM

 - 8+ years of product marketing experience in B2B SaaS, with a track record of owning pricing and go-to-market strategy for a core product line

 - Experience with AI-driven products and how they change buyer behavior and competitive dynamics

 - Proven experience designing and launching pricing and packaging changes that measurably moved revenue or adoption

 - Strong executive presence and the ability to influence senior stakeholders across all functions

 - Deep experience building competitive positioning and messaging that sales and customer-facing teams rely on day to day

 - A strategic mindset paired with the ability to get into the details when a launch or pricing change needs it

 - Excellent written and verbal communication skills, comfortable presenting to executive leadership and customers alike

 - Experience mentoring or informally leading other product marketers or GTM professionals

 - Experience in the proposal, RFP, or sales enablement space is a plus
