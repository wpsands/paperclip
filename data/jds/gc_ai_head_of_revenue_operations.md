ABOUT THE ROLE

We are seeking a Head of Revenue Operations to build and lead our revenue operations function from the ground up at GC AI. This role will report to Andrew Conley https://www.linkedin.com/in/andrewmconley/ (CRO). You will have unparalleled visibility into company-wide initiatives and the autonomy to shape the strategy and execution of marketing, sales, customer, and partner operations. You will be a hands-on operator and visionary strategist, responsible for hiring and building a high-performing team, establishing scalable processes, and selecting the right technology stack to drive revenue growth.

We’re looking for a self-starter who thrives in ambiguous, fast-paced environments, with deep expertise in revenue operations and a proven ability to go from 0 to 1. Ideally, you’ve built revenue operations functions or led transformative initiatives independently, not just supported senior leaders, and are passionate about aligning cross-functional teams to deliver measurable outcomes in a legal tech, AI-driven market.

WHAT YOU'LL DO

 - Build Revenue Operations from the Ground Up: Design and implement a comprehensive revenue operations strategy, encompassing marketing, sales, customer, and partner operations, to optimize the end-to-end revenue lifecycle and drive sustainable growth.

 - Hire and Lead a Team: Recruit, onboard, and mentor a high-performing revenue operations team, fostering a culture of collaboration, accountability, and excellence.

 - Create Processes and Structure: Develop and document scalable processes for demand generation, sales forecasting, pipeline management, customer retention, and partner strategy

 - Select and Implement Technology: Identify, acquire, and integrate a best-in-class technology stack to support revenue operations and enhance team efficiency.

 - Drive Cross-Functional Alignment: Partner with Sales, Marketing, Product, Customer Success, and Partnerships teams to align strategies, streamline workflows, and ensure consistent messaging and execution for legal industry buyers.

 - Optimize Revenue Performance: Translate go-to-market initiatives into actionable programs that fuel pipeline growth, accelerate deal cycles, improve win rates, and maximize customer lifetime value.

 - Measure and Report Impact: Define and track KPIs (e.g., revenue growth, pipeline velocity, customer retention rates, partner contribution) to assess performance, identify bottlenecks, and drive data-driven decisions.

 - Act as a Strategic Doer: Balance hands-on execution (e.g., building playbooks, configuring tools, training teams) with strategic leadership to set the vision and roadmap for revenue operations.

WHAT YOU’VE DONE

 - 7+ years of experience in revenue operations, sales operations, marketing operations, or related roles, with at least 3 years in a leadership position.

 - Proven track record of building revenue operations functions or leading transformative initiatives from scratch in a B2B SaaS environment, ideally in legal tech or AI-driven software.

 - Experience recruiting, hiring, and managing cross-functional teams in a high-growth startup or scale-up.

 - Demonstrated ability to design and implement processes, structures, and technology stacks for marketing, sales, customer, and partner operations.

 - Exceptional communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to align diverse stakeholders and drive consensus in ambiguous settings.

 - Strong analytical mindset, with expertise in defining KPIs, leveraging data, and optimizing revenue performance.

 - Self-starter mentality, with a history of thriving in fast-paced environments and balancing strategic planning with tactical execution.
