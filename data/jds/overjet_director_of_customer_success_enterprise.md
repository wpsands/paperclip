The Role

As Director of Customer Success, Enterprise at Overjet, you will lead Customer Success for some of our largest, most strategic, and most complex customers, with a primary focus on Dental Support Organizations (DSOs).

You will lead and develop a team of Customer Success Managers while also serving as a senior strategic partner to key enterprise customers. This is a hands-on leadership role responsible for driving retention, adoption, expansion, and long-term customer value across complex, multi-location organizations.

You will bring greater structure and consistency to Overjet’s enterprise Customer Success motion by building scalable playbooks, strengthening executive relationships, and using customer and business data to proactively identify risk and growth opportunities.

Responsibilities 

 - Drive Retention & Expansion: Own net revenue retention, churn reduction, product adoption, and expansion across Overjet’s strategic enterprise and DSO customers.

 - Lead & Develop the Team: Recruit, coach, and manage a high-performing Customer Success team with clear goals, accountability, and performance expectations.

 - Build the Enterprise CS Playbook: Develop scalable customer success strategies, engagement models, health frameworks, and risk mitigation plans for the enterprise segment.

 - Own the Customer Journey: Ensure strong execution across onboarding, adoption, renewal, and expansion, with proactive management of customer health, risk, and growth opportunities.

 - Build Strategic Customer Relationships: Develop trusted relationships with executive, clinical, and operational stakeholders and ensure customers realize measurable value from Overjet.

 - Partner Cross-Functionally: Work closely with Sales, Product, Marketing, and other teams to improve the customer experience, surface customer insights, and influence business priorities.

 - Drive Visibility & Accountability: Maintain accurate forecasts and reporting on retention, renewals, customer health, and expansion, and lead quarterly complaint trend reviews to support medical device regulatory compliance.

Qualifications 

 - 7+ years of experience in Customer Success, Account Management, Strategic Accounts, or a related customer-facing function in B2B SaaS.

 - 3+ years of experience leading and developing Customer Success or Account Management teams.

 - Proven experience managing complex enterprise or strategic customers with multiple stakeholders and executive-level relationships.

 - Demonstrated success driving retention, renewals, adoption, and expansion across a meaningful customer portfolio.

 - Strong executive presence, commercial judgment, and ability to navigate complex customer conversations and escalations.

 - Experience building or improving Customer Success processes, playbooks, metrics, and operating rhythms in a scaling organization.

 - Strong analytical skills and ability to use customer and business data to identify risk, diagnose problems, and drive action.

 - Experience using AI, automation, and customer success tools to improve team efficiency, customer insights, and decision-making.

 - Experience with DSOs, multi-location healthcare organizations, dental technology, or vertical SaaS is strongly preferred.
