ABOUT THE ROLE

In-house lawyers find GC AI in ways most SaaS companies would envy. They take our AI classes, meet us at tradeshows and upscale events, watch our webinars, read our newsletter, join our Slack community, listen to CZ and Friends, and tell their general counsel friends. Then they start a trial, buy seats, and bring their whole team (and increasingly, their colleagues outside legal). That flywheel works. Now we need someone to turn it into a predictable, scalable pipeline engine that reaches the next several thousand legal teams, from fast-growing startups to Fortune 500 companies.

We are hiring our Director of Demand Generation to own GC AI's pipeline number. You will build and run the programs that create qualified opportunities across self-serve, mid-market, enterprise, and expansion motions. You will also build the team, systems, and operating rhythm to scale them. You'll partner daily with Sales, our SDR team, Revenue Operations, Marketing Operations, Product Marketing, Brand, and Customer Success.

This role reports to the CMO and combines strategic leadership with hands-on execution. You'll set the plan in the morning and ship the campaign in the afternoon.

WHAT YOU'LL DO

Own the Pipeline Number

 - Own marketing-sourced and marketing-influenced pipeline targets, with full accountability for volume, quality, and velocity

 - Build the demand plan with Sales, including pipeline coverage targets by segment and a clear channel-level forecast

 - Report weekly on pipeline, conversion, and spend efficiency to leadership; run a regular pipeline review with Sales leadership

Lead the Demand Generation Team

 - Hire, mentor, and develop demand generation marketers as the function grows

 - Set the operating rhythm, quality bar, and playbooks for the next stage of growth

Build the Full-Funnel Engine

 - Build segmentation and tiered account-based programs for enterprise and strategic accounts, coordinated with Sales and SDR plays

 - Design and run integrated campaigns end-to-end: audience, offer, landing page, copy, paid amplification, nurture, sales follow-up, and post-campaign analysis

 - Define demand generation KPIs

 - Partner with Marketing Operations, RevOps, and GTM Engineering on processes and attribution so every program connects pipeline and revenue

Orchestrate pipeline efforts across teams

 - Collaborate with field marketing to drive pipeline from tradeshows and executive events

 - Partner with the digital acquisition leader on paid strategy and budget across channels

 - Work with our partners on co-marketing, integrations, launches, and joint programs

 - Work closely with our sellers on account selection, messaging, sequencing, and follow-up

 - Partner with Customer Success on expansion campaigns

 - 8+ years in B2B demand generation, growth, or performance marketing, including 2+ years leading a team or function at a high-growth software company

 - A record of owning a pipeline number and hitting it, and you can walk us through the numbers behind every decision

 - Experience running demand across both product-led (trial, self-serve) and sales-led motions, from mid-market to enterprise
