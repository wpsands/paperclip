## Job description
Who we are
Hi there 👋 We’re Ethena, compliance training that customizes itself to you. Ethena combines top-rated training content with intelligent AI that builds and adapts training to reflect your company’s unique brand, roles, policies, and risks, so it feels like your team, whether that’s hard hats or virtual meetings. From auto-assigning courses based on HRIS data to transforming your policies into interactive training in minutes, Ethena helps People, Legal, and Compliance teams launch in days (not months) and cut learner seat time by up to 60% with adaptive learning paths. Ethena supports 1M+ learners across 2,000+ organizations, with a 93% learner approval rating and 96% customer renewal rate. Over the past several years, we’ve delivered consistent, durable growth, including 120%+ net dollar retention, 95%+ gross dollar retention, and strong enterprise expansion across industries. Ethena has become a go-to compliance partner for modern, global organizations.

Beyond training, Ethena unifies core compliance tools, Hotline & Case Management, Policy Bot, and Phishing Simulator, into a single platform, with built-in audit trails and compliance controls teams can trust.

Trusted by teams at companies like Genesco, Asana, Pinterest, and Synaptics, it’s a great time to join us.

How we work
We’re a team that values diversity in all its forms and loves adding new perspectives. We ask thoughtful questions, challenge assumptions, and build with empathy, for customers and for each other. We care about craft, we love feedback, and we take ownership. We also embrace humor (yes, the puns are real), because doing serious work doesn’t have to feel so serious.

Our workforce is fully remote, and your personal working hours can be based on your own timezone. All team-wide meetings are scheduled to be inclusive of all North American time zones.

## Who We're Looking For

At Ethena, we’re an AI platform that’s revolutionizing the Compliance space. Our product and engineering teams ship fast—really fast. Build cycles are shrinking, product offerings are expanding, and our roadmap evolves to meet the needs of a changing workplace.

That’s where you come in.

We’re looking for a 
Head of Product Marketing
 to serve as the vital bridge between our Product team and the rest of the world. Reporting directly to our Product-facing 
Co-Founder and CTO
, Anne Solmssen, you will be the chief storyteller, positioning strategist, and launch maestro for everything we build. Our buyers are compliance, HR, and legal leaders. Your job is to turn what we build into a compelling story. You’ll spend time with key buyers every week on demo calls, in customer conversations, and on webinars. Then you’ll bring what you hear straight back to the Product team to help shape what we build next.

This is not a role where you oversee strategy from a distance. You’ll be on calls helping us win deals and building the assets yourself.

## What You'll Do

Be the product voice in the room on our most strategic deals

Show buyers where the platform is going; demo vibecode prototypes to paint a picture of the future

Give compliance, HR, and legal leaders clear answers to their toughest questions

Keep our product story sharp and buyer-first across every product line

Help us operationalize cross-functional efforts to win key deals

Bring what you hear on calls back to the Product team to help shape our roadmap

Translate upcoming features into plain language for buyers and internal teams

Build ad hoc assets (such as decks, one-sheets, prototypes, and Looms) to support key product launches

## What you'll bring

The ability to tell a clear story, and to field live questions from buyers at the right zoom level

Impeccable internal communication skills that align cross functional teams around clear narratives

Startup experience at our stage where you wore multiple hats, worked with little structure, and figured things out yourself

A track record of taking complex B2B SaaS products to market by anchoring every story in the customer’s pain points

Comfort on live calls with senior buyers and a strong working partnership with commercial teams

Ability to use AI to increase your output efficiency, while maintaining a high quality bar

High agency and comfort with ambiguity in a fast-moving, high-build environment

A sense of humor and real curiosity about how our customers work
