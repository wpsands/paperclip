## Job description
Cloudinary is the Image and Video API platform trusted by millions of developers and over 10,000 companies worldwide. Our powerful tools fuel websites to be faster, richer in user engagement, and breakthrough creativity. With a growing suite of products that support everything from developer efficiency to brand storytelling, Cloudinary is becoming the visual layer of the web.

Cloudinary has a globally distributed team across North America, EMEA, and Asia-Pacific, united by a shared commitment to building smart, scalable technology and a culture rooted in humility, curiosity, and collaboration. At Cloudinary, we celebrate Life & Work. We believe in healthy growth, working with purpose, and creating meaningful value. If you’re looking for a place where your voice matters and your work truly makes an impact, this may be the opportunity for you.

## Responsibilities:

• Own and evolve the operating model and cadence for all New Business motions, including standards, agendas, decision rights, and priorities.

• Lead forecast readiness and opportunity inspection, giving leaders an accurate, timely view of performance, risk, upside, stage progression, qualification, next steps, close dates, and opportunity health.

• Improve pipeline hygiene, opportunity completeness, and Salesforce data quality; combine Account Executive, Sales Engineer, and system signals to surface deal risk, blockers, and forecast changes early.

• Build and curate reporting that drives decisions, consolidating redundant dashboards into clear, useful views for New Business leaders and the field.

• Lead cross-functional initiatives involving CRM, CPQ, RFPs, pricing, and other GTM systems and processes from problem definition through delivery and adoption.

• Translate field needs into clear requirements, acceptance criteria, and success measures; coordinate owners, make tradeoffs visible, remove blockers, and escalate decisions with context.

• Partner with Marketing, Enablement, and Channels & Alliances to align handoffs and shared workflows, and convert field feedback into a prioritized improvement backlog.

• Create durable playbooks and inspection mechanisms; measure adoption, data quality, forecast accuracy, and process performance, and use the evidence to improve the operating model.

## About You:

• Bachelor's degree or equivalent practical experience, plus 10+ years of progressive experience in Business Operations, Sales Strategy, or related roles in B2B SaaS or technology companies.

• Experience owning or materially shaping the operating model for New Business or enterprise sales motions.

• Strong experience with forecasting, pipeline management, opportunity inspection, and revenue-process discipline.

• Salesforce experience and familiarity with GTM systems such as CPQ and RFP tools, are highly desired. 

• A proven ability to lead complex, cross-functional initiatives from requirements through delivery, adoption, and measurable outcomes.

• Strong analytical and data-storytelling skills, with the judgment to distinguish useful decision support from reporting noise.

• Exceptional stakeholder-management, written, and verbal communication skills, with the ability to create alignment and accountability without direct authority.

• Comfort moving between executive-level framing and detailed execution, with a high bar for clarity, urgency, organization, and follow-through.

## Nice to Have:

• Experience supporting global, technical, or developer-focused sales organizations.

• Experience simplifying and increasing efficiencies of GTM processes.

• Familiarity with Cloudinary's market, including APIs, cloud technologies, digital asset management, or media workflows.
